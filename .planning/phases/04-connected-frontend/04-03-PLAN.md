| T-04-08 | Denial of service | malformed manifest (missing `steps`/`error`) crashing status compute | mitigate | `computeStepStatus` guards with `manifest?.steps?.[stepId]` and returns `idle` when absent; error branch only reads `stepMeta.error` after the existence check. |

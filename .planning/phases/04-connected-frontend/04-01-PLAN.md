<objective>
Slice: "A failed step is recorded as failed." When any pipeline step throws, the manifest's `steps[stepId]` entry gains `{ error: <message>, failedAt: <ISO timestamp> }` so the frontend (Plan 03) can render an `error` state instead of leaving the step stuck as `running` or mislabeled `done`. Implements REL-01 (D-06).

Purpose: REL-01 is the data contract Plan 03's UI-03 error indicator reads from. Without it the UI has no way to distinguish "still running" from "crashed".
Output: Error-capture in `step-texts`, `step-images`, and the `runLocally` fire-and-forget runner, plus a node:test contract proving it.
</objective>
